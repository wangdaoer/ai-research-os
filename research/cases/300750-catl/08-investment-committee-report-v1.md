# Investment Committee Report v1.0

## Company
CATL (300750.SZ)

## Research Conclusion

A high-quality company does not automatically imply an attractive investment opportunity.

## Investment Thesis

1. Leading competitive position in battery technology and manufacturing.
2. Long-term energy transition provides structural demand support.
3. Execution capability remains a key advantage.

## Key Debate

The central question is not whether the company is excellent, but whether current valuation sufficiently compensates for future uncertainty.

## Bull Case

- Strong storage growth
- Stable margins
- Successful global expansion

## Base Case

- Moderate growth
- Increasing competition
- Stable but lower profitability

## Bear Case

- Price competition
- Margin compression
- Slower demand growth

## Current Action

WATCH.

Await valuation and market expectation analysis before increasing exposure.

## Review Items

- Earnings trajectory
- Margin trend
- Market share
- Industry pricing
- Overseas expansion progress
